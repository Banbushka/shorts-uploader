Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the application Shorts Uploader ("the App") handles data. The App is a personal-use tool operated solely by its owner.

Who operates the App

The App is operated by an individual for personal use. Contact: diego.c.velarde@gmail.com.

What the App does

The App uploads video files to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3. It acts only on the owner's own Google account, authorised by the owner.

Google user data the App accesses

The App requests the youtube.upload permission, which allows it to upload videos to the authenticated user's own YouTube channel. The App does not read, collect, or process data belonging to any other user.

How data is used and stored

The access granted is used exclusively to upload the owner's own video content. The App does not sell, share, or transfer any Google user data to third parties. Authentication tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are not shared.

Limited Use disclosure

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and revocation

No third-party user data is retained. The owner may revoke the App's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated. Changes will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
